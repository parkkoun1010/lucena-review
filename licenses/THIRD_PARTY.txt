  License: CC BY-SA 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
  Changed: fill colors, outline colors and shadows. The changed images use the same license.
  See ../pieces/ATTRIBUTION.md.
"Sculpted" chess pieces: drawn for this project.
Opening names: lichess-org/chess-openings, CC0 1.0, https://github.com/lichess-org/chess-openings
Sample games: public Lichess games (CC0), player names replaced with "White" and "Black".
